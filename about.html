About SAMG

Quality is what we pursue, we know what we do

SAMG is a manpower supplying and contracting company based in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, delivering professional, comprehensive and cost-effective solutions to industrial projects of every scale. Our services span technical manpower supply, and the construction and maintenance of small- and large-scale industrial facilities.

We are committed to providing high-quality plant maintenance services built on advanced technological innovation and years of cumulative field experience. Our skilled manpower strives to deliver the highest-quality solutions to every customer, with extensive experience across the commercial, industrial and electrical power sectors in Saudi Arabia.

  • Reliable advice and quality workmanship on every site
  • Cost-efficient delivery, timed to the client's schedule
  • Extensive experience in commercial, industrial & power sectors
